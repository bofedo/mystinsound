About

Between music, branding
and spatial sound.

My work sits between music creation, marketing strategy and sonic branding research. Alongside my marketing work, I develop personal compositions, sound ideas for visual media and spatial audio experiments in Dolby Atmos and Ambisonics workflows.

I do not position MystinSound as a large commercial studio. It is more of a personal and project-based space where I can connect atmosphere, story, emotion and sonic detail — from film-inspired compositions to sound ideas for brands and experimental projects.

The research side continues separately through DoReMiFo, a project focused on sonic branding, sound perception and the relationship between acoustic properties and emotion.
